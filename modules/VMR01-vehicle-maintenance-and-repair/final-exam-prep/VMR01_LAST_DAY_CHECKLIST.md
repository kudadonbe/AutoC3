# VMR01 Last Day Checklist

Use this the day before the final exam.

## 60-Minute Fast Revision

### First 15 Minutes - Engine and Safety

- Explain the four-stroke cycle without looking.
- Compare petrol and diesel ignition.
- List main engine components and their functions.
- Say the workshop safety rules aloud.
- Explain safe vehicle lifting with jack stands.

### Next 15 Minutes - Service Systems

- A/C cycle: compressor, condenser, receiver/dryer or accumulator, expansion valve/orifice tube, evaporator.
- Petrol fuel system components.
- Diesel fuel system components.
- Ignition primary and secondary circuits.
- OBD code meaning and diagnostic use.

### Next 15 Minutes - Chassis Systems

- Steering components and HPS vs EPS.
- Brake hydraulic operation.
- ABS operation.
- Suspension components and types.
- Differential and drivetrain layouts.

### Final 15 Minutes - Transmission and Faults

- Manual transmission parts and clutch function.
- Gear ratio formula.
- Automatic transmission components.
- Torque converter and ATF functions.
- Common fault symptoms: misfire, no crank, brake noise, weak A/C, heavy steering, worn suspension.

## Topics You Must Be Able To Draw or Label

- Four-stroke engine cycle.

- Petrol fuel system flow.

- Ignition primary/secondary circuit.
- Hydraulic brake system.

- Automatic transmission torque converter.

## Common Exam Command Words

- Define: give a short meaning.
- List: give points only.
- Explain: describe how or why.
- Compare: show similarities and differences.
- Describe procedure: write steps in correct order.
- Diagnose: connect symptom to likely cause and test.

## Memory Hooks

- Four strokes: Intake, Compression, Power, Exhaust.
- A/C cycle: Compress, Condense, Dry/Store, Expand, Evaporate.
- Brake operation: Pedal, pressure, friction, stop.
- Starting system: Battery, switch, solenoid, starter, flywheel.
- OBD systems: P powertrain, B body, C chassis, U network.
- Suspension job: support, absorb, contact, control.

## Practical Answer Formula

When asked a service or inspection question, answer in this order:

1. Safety first.
2. Confirm customer complaint.
3. Visual inspection.
4. Check fluid/electrical/mechanical condition.
5. Test or scan if required.
6. Identify likely cause.
7. Service/repair using correct procedure.
8. Recheck operation.
9. Record job details.
