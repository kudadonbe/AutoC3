# VMR01 Practice Questions

Use these for active recall. Try answering before checking the answer.

## Short Answer

1. What is the function of an internal combustion engine?
Answer: It converts chemical energy in fuel into mechanical energy through combustion inside the cylinder.

2. List the four strokes of a four-stroke engine in order.
Answer: Intake, compression, power, exhaust.

3. Which stroke produces power?
Answer: Power/expansion stroke.

4. What is the main difference between petrol and diesel ignition?
Answer: Petrol uses spark ignition; diesel uses heat from compression ignition.

5. Name five workshop safety precautions.
Answer: Wear PPE, keep area clean, ventilate workspace, use correct tools, chock/support vehicle, clean spills, avoid hot/moving parts.

6. Why must jack stands be used after lifting a vehicle?
Answer: A jack can fail or move; jack stands safely support the vehicle.

7. What information should be recorded during vehicle intake?
Answer: Customer details, vehicle details, complaint/job request, visible damage, contact method, and basic checks.

8. Why should wheels be cleaned with separate brushes?
Answer: Brake dust and grit can scratch paint if transferred to body panels.

9. Why should a vehicle not be air dried after washing?
Answer: It can leave water spots and mineral marks.

10. What is engine inspection?
Answer: A detailed check of engine condition, leaks, wear, fluids, noises, performance, and fault codes.

11. Name four engine fluids checked during inspection.
Answer: Engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, transmission fluid, power steering fluid where applicable.

12. What does an OBD scanner do?
Answer: Reads diagnostic trouble codes and vehicle data from control modules.

13. List the A/C refrigeration cycle in order.
Answer: Compressor, condenser, receiver/dryer or accumulator, expansion valve/orifice tube, evaporator, compressor.

14. What is the function of the A/C compressor?
Answer: Compresses low-pressure gas into high-pressure, high-temperature gas.

15. What is the function of the condenser?
Answer: Releases heat and changes refrigerant from gas to liquid.

16. Name three causes of warm air from A/C vents.
Answer: Low refrigerant, refrigerant leak, faulty compressor, blocked condenser, electrical fault.

17. Name three A/C leak detection methods.
Answer: UV dye, electronic sniffer, soapy water, visual inspection, nitrogen pressure test.

18. What is the function of a petrol fuel pump?
Answer: Pumps petrol from tank to engine/fuel injectors under pressure.

19. What are symptoms of a clogged fuel filter?
Answer: Hard starting, hesitation, loss of power, stalling, poor acceleration.

20. What is direct injection?
Answer: Fuel is injected directly into the combustion chamber.

21. Why is water in diesel fuel dangerous?
Answer: It can damage high-pressure pump/injectors and cause poor combustion/corrosion.

22. What is the function of a diesel lift pump?
Answer: Transfers fuel from tank to high-pressure pump.

23. What is common rail diesel injection?
Answer: A high-pressure rail supplies electronically controlled injectors.

24. What does the ECU do in a modern diesel system?
Answer: Receives sensor inputs, calculates fuel control, and commands actuators/injectors.

25. What is the function of an ignition coil?
Answer: Steps battery voltage up to high voltage for spark plugs.

26. What is the difference between primary and secondary ignition circuits?
Answer: Primary is low voltage control side; secondary is high voltage spark side.

27. Name four ignition system components.
Answer: Battery, ignition switch, coil, spark plug, distributor/module, ECU.

28. Name three ignition system types.
Answer: Breaker-point, electronic, distributorless, coil-on-plug.

29. What is the main function of the steering system?
Answer: Converts driver input into directional wheel movement.

30. Name two common steering gear types.
Answer: Rack and pinion; recirculating ball.

31. Compare HPS and EPS.
Answer: HPS uses hydraulic pressure from an engine-driven pump; EPS uses an electric motor and is more efficient.

32. What does a tie rod do?
Answer: Connects steering gear/linkage to steering knuckle to turn wheels.

33. Why is a transmission needed?
Answer: To match engine RPM to vehicle speed/load and provide suitable torque.

34. What is the formula for gear ratio?
Answer: Driven gear teeth divided by driving gear teeth.

35. What does a clutch do?
Answer: Connects and disconnects engine power from the transmission.

36. What are the basic manual transmission parts?
Answer: Clutch, gearbox, input shaft, output shaft, gears, shift mechanism.

37. What replaces the clutch in an automatic transmission?
Answer: Torque converter.

38. Name three torque converter parts.
Answer: Impeller/pump, turbine, stator.

39. What does ATF do?
Answer: Transfers power, provides hydraulic pressure, lubricates, cools, and controls friction.

40. What is the function of a valve body?
Answer: Directs pressurized ATF to control clutches, bands, and gear shifts.

41. What is the function of brakes?
Answer: Convert vehicle kinetic energy into heat through friction to slow or stop the vehicle.

42. Name five brake system components.
Answer: Pedal, booster, master cylinder, lines, fluid, caliper, pads, disc, drum, wheel cylinder.

43. How does hydraulic braking work?
Answer: Pedal force creates fluid pressure in master cylinder, which applies brake components at wheels.

44. What does ABS prevent?
Answer: Wheel lock during hard braking.

45. What are symptoms of worn brake pads?
Answer: Squealing, grinding, reduced braking, warning light where fitted.

46. What is the function of a starter solenoid?
Answer: Acts as high-current relay and engages starter drive with flywheel.

47. What cranking speed is commonly needed to start an engine?
Answer: About 200-300 RPM.

48. What does DTC code letter P mean?
Answer: Powertrain.

49. What does a differential do?
Answer: Allows drive wheels to turn at different speeds while transmitting torque.

50. Compare AWD and 4WD.
Answer: AWD automatically varies power for on-road traction; 4WD is usually driver-selectable and suited to off-road/low traction.

51. What is the function of a suspension system?
Answer: Supports vehicle weight, absorbs shocks, keeps tires on road, improves comfort and control.

52. What does a shock absorber do?
Answer: Controls spring movement and prevents bouncing.

53. What is a MacPherson strut?
Answer: A common independent suspension design using a strut and lower control arm.

54. Name four signs of suspension faults.
Answer: Noise over bumps, uneven tire wear, vehicle pulling, excessive bounce, leaking shock, poor handling.

## Scenario Questions

1. A vehicle has a soft brake pedal. What should you inspect first?
Answer: Brake fluid level, leaks, air in system, master cylinder, brake lines/hoses.

2. A vehicle cranks slowly and dashboard lights dim. Likely cause?
Answer: Weak battery or poor battery terminal/ground connection.

3. A petrol engine misfires under acceleration. What systems may be checked?
Answer: Spark plugs, ignition coil, fuel filter, injectors, air intake, OBD codes.

4. A diesel vehicle starts then stalls after filter replacement. Likely cause?
Answer: Air trapped in diesel fuel system; bleeding/priming may be required.

5. A/C blows cold at first then weak airflow. What might be wrong?
Answer: Cabin filter blocked, evaporator icing, blower issue, vent blockage.

6. Steering is heavy at low speed in a hydraulic system. What should be checked?
Answer: Power steering fluid level, belt, pump, leaks, steering rack.

7. ABS light stays on after start. What is the likely next diagnostic step?
Answer: Scan ABS module for fault codes and inspect wheel speed sensors/wiring.

8. Vehicle bounces several times after pressing down on fender. Likely fault?
Answer: Weak shock absorber/strut.

9. Grinding noise while braking. What is likely?
Answer: Brake pads worn down causing metal-to-metal contact with rotor/disc.

10. Vehicle vibrates during braking. What should be inspected?
Answer: Warped/uneven brake rotors, pad condition, wheel bearings, suspension.
