## What To Revise First

### AEP01

- Four-stroke cycle.
- Petrol vs diesel.
- Engine component names/functions.
- QG15DE key specifications.
- Engine disassembly sequence.
- Timing chain alignment.
- Engine measurements and clearances.
- Common engine faults, symptoms, causes, rectification.

### BWP01

- Workshop safety and PPE.
- Manual lifting.
- Hand tool categories.
- Measuring and marking tools.

- Chisels and hammers.
- Drilling machine parts.
- Taps, dies, countersinking.
- Drilling safety and cutting oil.

### VMR01

- Vehicle intake/job card.
- Safe moving, parking, and lifting.
- Engine inspection/service.
- A/C system.
- Petrol and diesel fuel systems.
- Ignition system.

## 90-Minute Revision Plan

### 0-30 Minutes: AEP01

- Draw and explain four-stroke cycle.
- List engine components and functions.
- Recite QG15DE specs: 4-cylinder inline, DOHC, 1497 cm3, 1-3-4-2 firing order, 9.9:1 compression ratio, 1.0-1.1 mm plug gap.
- Explain engine disassembly and assembly safety.
- Review engine fault table: starting, overheating, lubrication, smoke, misfire.

### 30-50 Minutes: BWP01

- List PPE.
- Explain safe lifting.
- Group hand tools by purpose.
- Explain measuring/marking tools.
- Review drill press safety.
- Explain tap, die, and countersink.

### 50-90 Minutes: VMR01

- Revise A/C cycle.
- Revise petrol/diesel fuel systems.
- Revise ignition circuit.
- Revise steering HPS vs EPS.
- Revise manual/automatic transmission.
- Revise brakes and ABS.
- Revise OBD and starting system.
- Revise differential and suspension.

## Must Be Able To Explain Without Notes

- Intake, compression, power, exhaust.
- Petrol spark ignition vs diesel compression ignition.
- Engine disassembly safety and correct ordering.
- Why timing marks matter.
- Why torque specifications matter.
- Safe manual lifting.
- Correct use of hacksaw, file, drill, tap, and die.
- A/C refrigerant flow.
- Hydraulic brake operation.
- ABS operation.
- Gear ratio.
- Torque converter.
- OBD code purpose.
- Differential.
- Shock absorber/strut function.

## Final Exam Answer Formula

Use this for most answers:

1. Define the system/component.
2. State its function.
3. Explain operation in order.
4. State inspection/service checks.
5. Give common faults and rectification.
6. Mention safety and correct tools.
