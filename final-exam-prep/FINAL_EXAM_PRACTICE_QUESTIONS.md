# Final Exam Practice Questions: AEP01, BWP01, VMR01

Try answering first, then check the answer.

## AEP01 Questions

1. What is the function of an internal combustion engine?
Answer: It converts chemical energy in fuel into mechanical energy.

2. List the four strokes in order.
Answer: Intake, compression, power, exhaust.

3. What is the only power-producing stroke?
Answer: Power/expansion stroke.

4. Compare petrol and diesel ignition.
Answer: Petrol uses spark ignition; diesel uses compression ignition.

5. Name ten main engine components.
Answer: Block, cylinder head, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, camshaft, valves, timing chain/belt, spark plug/injector, oil pump, water pump, radiator, flywheel.

6. What does the crankshaft do?
Answer: Converts piston reciprocating motion into rotary motion.

7. What does the timing chain do?
Answer: Synchronizes crankshaft and camshaft so valves open at the correct time.

8. Why should No. 1 piston be set to TDC before timing chain removal?
Answer: To preserve valve timing reference and make correct reassembly possible.

9. Why must rods, caps, bearings, and pistons not be mixed?
Answer: They wear together and must return to their original positions.

10. What safety steps are needed before engine disassembly?
Answer: Secure engine, drain fluids, wear PPE, prepare clean area, label parts, use correct tools.

11. What is the firing order of the Nissan QG15DE?
Answer: 1-3-4-2.

12. What is the QG15DE spark plug gap?
Answer: 1.0-1.1 mm.

13. What is the QG15DE standard compression pressure?
Answer: 1,372 kPa / 198.9 psi at 350 rpm.

14. What is the QG15DE minimum compression pressure?
Answer: 1,176 kPa / 170.5 psi.

15. What is checked with a feeler gauge?
Answer: Small clearances such as valve clearance or ring gaps depending on procedure.

16. What does blue exhaust smoke usually indicate?
Answer: Oil burning.

17. What does black exhaust smoke usually indicate?
Answer: Rich mixture or excess fuel.

18. What does white smoke usually indicate?
Answer: Coolant entering combustion chamber or condensation.

19. Name five engine diagnostic tools.
Answer: Compression tester, vacuum gauge, multimeter, OBD-II scanner, stethoscope, micrometer, bore gauge.

20. What can cause engine overheating?
Answer: Low coolant, radiator leak/blockage, thermostat fault, water pump fault, fan fault.

## BWP01 Questions

1. What is workshop safety?
Answer: Practices, rules, and procedures used to prevent injury and damage in a workshop.

2. Name six PPE items.
Answer: Safety glasses, gloves, safety shoes, helmet, hearing protection, face shield, respirator, overalls.

3. Give the correct manual lifting method.
Answer: Stand close, bend knees, keep back straight, grip firmly, lift smoothly with legs, keep load close, avoid twisting.

4. Name three injuries caused by poor manual lifting.
Answer: Muscle strain, herniated disc, hernia, tendinitis, bursitis, stress injury.

5. What is a hacksaw used for?
Answer: Cutting metal or plastic rods/pipes.

6. What is a bench vice used for?
Answer: Holding a workpiece securely.

7. What are files used for?
Answer: Smoothing, shaping, and finishing material.

8. Why must a file have a handle?
Answer: To prevent hand injury from the tang and improve control.

9. What does a try square check?
Answer: Right angles/squareness.

10. What does a scriber do?
Answer: Marks lines on metal or other material.

11. What are taps used for?
Answer: Cutting internal threads.

12. What are dies used for?
Answer: Cutting external threads.

13. What is countersinking?
Answer: Making a conical opening so a countersunk screw head sits flush.

14. Why is cutting oil used when drilling/tapping metal?
Answer: Reduces heat, friction, and tool wear; improves finish.

15. Name five drilling machine parts.
Answer: Bed, pillar, table, motor, pulley, spindle, chuck, drill bit, hand wheel.

16. What safety check must be done before starting a drill press?
Answer: Remove chuck key and clamp workpiece securely.

17. Why do larger drill bits require lower RPM?
Answer: Their cutting edge travels faster at the same RPM and can overheat.

18. What is a mallet used for?
Answer: Striking without damaging delicate material as much as a steel hammer.

19. Why should mushroomed chisel heads not be used?
Answer: They can chip and cause eye or body injury.

20. What is the purpose of a clamp?
Answer: Temporarily holds work securely during cutting, drilling, gluing, or assembly.

## VMR01 Questions

Use these together with `modules/VMR01-vehicle-maintenance-and-repair/final-exam-prep/VMR01_PRACTICE_QUESTIONS.md`.

1. What information goes on a job card?
Answer: Customer details, vehicle details, complaint, work requested, inspection findings, parts/labor, technician, date, and final checks.

2. Why must a vehicle be parked on level ground before lifting?
Answer: To prevent rolling or unstable lifting.

3. What is the A/C cycle order?
Answer: Compressor, condenser, receiver/dryer or accumulator, expansion valve/orifice tube, evaporator, compressor.

4. What causes weak A/C airflow?
Answer: Clogged cabin filter, blower fault, blocked vents, evaporator issue.

5. What is the function of a fuel filter?
Answer: Removes dirt and contaminants from fuel.

6. What is the function of a diesel high-pressure fuel pump?
Answer: Raises fuel pressure for injection.

7. What does an ignition coil do?
Answer: Steps battery voltage up to high voltage for spark plugs.

8. Compare hydraulic and electric power steering.
Answer: Hydraulic uses pump/fluid pressure; electric uses a motor and control module.

9. What is the formula for gear ratio?
Answer: Driven gear teeth divided by driving gear teeth.

10. What does a torque converter do?
Answer: Transfers engine power to automatic transmission using fluid and allows smooth take-off.

11. What does ATF do?
Answer: Transfers power, provides hydraulic pressure, lubricates, cools, and controls friction.

12. How does hydraulic braking work?
Answer: Pedal force creates fluid pressure that applies calipers/wheel cylinders.

13. What does ABS prevent?
Answer: Wheel lock during hard braking.

14. What causes a soft brake pedal?
Answer: Air in brake system, fluid leak, low fluid, master cylinder fault.

15. What does an OBD-II scanner read?
Answer: Diagnostic trouble codes and live data from vehicle control modules.

16. What does a starter solenoid do?
Answer: Acts as high-current relay and engages starter pinion.

17. What does a differential do?
Answer: Allows drive wheels to rotate at different speeds while transmitting torque.

18. Compare AWD and 4WD.
Answer: AWD automatically varies power for on-road traction; 4WD is usually selectable for off-road/low-traction use.

19. What does a shock absorber do?
Answer: Controls spring movement and prevents bouncing.

20. Name four suspension components.
Answer: Springs, shocks, struts, control arms, sway bar, ball joints, bushings.

## Scenario Practice

1. Engine cranks but will not start. What do you check?
Answer: Battery/starter are working, then check fuel, spark, compression, air intake, OBD codes.

2. Engine overheats after short drive. What do you check?
Answer: Coolant level, leaks, radiator, thermostat, water pump, fan, hoses.

3. Vehicle makes grinding noise while braking. Likely cause?
Answer: Brake pads worn down causing metal-to-metal contact.

4. Steering is heavy at low speed. What do you inspect?
Answer: Power steering fluid/pump/belt/leaks for HPS, EPS motor/module/fuses for EPS, plus tires and steering linkage.

5. Vehicle bounces after going over bumps. Likely cause?
Answer: Weak shock absorbers or struts.

6. Diesel engine runs poorly after fuel filter change. Likely cause?
Answer: Air in fuel system or incorrect filter installation.

7. Drill bit overheats while drilling mild steel. What should you correct?
Answer: Reduce speed, use cutting oil, apply correct feed, check bit sharpness.

8. A chisel head is mushroomed. What should you do?
Answer: Do not use it; dress/repair or replace it.

9. After engine assembly, crankshaft is difficult to turn by hand. What might be wrong?
Answer: Incorrect bearing installation, wrong torque, insufficient lubrication, mixed caps, piston/ring issue, timing interference.

10. A/C has oily residue near a hose fitting. What does it suggest?
Answer: Refrigerant/oil leak at that point.
