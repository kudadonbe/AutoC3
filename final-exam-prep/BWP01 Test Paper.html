ChuckHolds the drill bit.

Table clamp / lockLocks the table position.

Safety: clamp the workpiece, remove chuck key, wear eye protection, use correct speed, use cutting oil for metal, keep hands away from rotating parts.

Section B

Vernier caliper parts: outside jaws, inside jaws, main scale, Vernier scale, depth rod, locking screw/fine adjustment. Uses: measuring outside, inside, depth, and accurate small dimensions.

Vernier readings: 12.10 mm, 24.16 mm, 36.06 mm.

Micrometer parts: frame, anvil, spindle, measuring faces, sleeve, thimble, ratchet stop, spindle lock.

Micrometer readings: 5.78 mm, 7.42 mm.

Section C

Center punching marks the drilling point, prevents drill-bit slipping, and helps drill the hole in the correct place.

Procedure: clean workpiece, measure position, mark using steel rule/scriber, check measurement, place punch on mark, hold vertical, strike lightly, check mark, deepen if correct.

Tap cuts internal thread in a hole. Die cuts external thread on a rod or bolt.

Tapping/dieing process: select correct tool, secure work, apply cutting oil, keep tool straight, turn forward and slightly backward to break chips, continue until thread is complete.

Section D

Tools: hacksaw cuts metal/plastic pipes and rods; handsaw cuts wood; screwdriver drives/removes screws; spanner turns nuts/bolts; pliers grip/twist/cut small parts or wires; hammer applies force; bench vice holds work firmly.

PPE: safety shoes protect feet; safety glasses protect eyes; gloves protect hands; ear protection protects hearing; face shield protects face; overalls protect body and clothing.

PPE is important because it reduces injury risk during workshop work.
